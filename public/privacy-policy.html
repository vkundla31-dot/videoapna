VideoApna Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

VideoApna is a video platform that allows users to discover, watch, upload, and share videos. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use VideoApna.

1. Information We Collect

VideoApna may collect information that you provide when creating or managing a profile, uploading videos, contacting support, or using platform features.

2. Profile Information

Users may provide information such as display name, profile photo, biography, preferred languages, and other profile information.

3. Uploaded Videos

Videos uploaded by users may be stored and displayed according to the visibility settings selected by the user. Users are responsible for having the necessary rights to upload and publish their content.

4. Usage and Recommendation Data

VideoApna may use information such as videos watched, viewing activity, likes, saves, searches, and similar interactions to improve video recommendations and platform functionality.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

VideoApna may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain sessions, and support platform functionality.

6. Advertising

VideoApna may display advertisements in the future. Advertising providers may collect certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. The specific advertising providers used by VideoApna may change over time.

7. Third-Party Video Sources

During the initial stage of VideoApna, videos may be displayed from third-party video platforms such as PeerTube. VideoApna does not claim ownership of third-party videos. Their availability and processing may be subject to the privacy policies and terms of their respective platforms.

8. Reports and Moderation

Users may report videos for reasons such as copyright concerns, inappropriate content, or other platform violations. Reports may be reviewed by VideoApna administrators and may result in content being restricted, made private, or removed from VideoApna.

9. Copyright and User Responsibility

Users should upload only content that they have the right or permission to publish. VideoApna may take appropriate action when valid copyright or ownership concerns are reported and verified.

10. Data Security

VideoApna takes reasonable measures to protect information used by the service. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Deletion

Users may be able to delete their uploaded videos and manage their profile information through available VideoApna features. Requests concerning account or personal information can also be sent to the support email below.

12. Children's Privacy

VideoApna is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

VideoApna may update this Privacy Policy when the service, features, advertising providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
